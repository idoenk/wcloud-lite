    Privacy

    The actual content of the file or copy-pasted text is not transmitted to any server in any form. Everything is processed on your computer directly, employing technologies in the browser known collectively as HTML5.

    No analytics or tracking scripts are included. Only the optional Wikipedia example fetches public article text directly from Wikipedia's MediaWiki API.
